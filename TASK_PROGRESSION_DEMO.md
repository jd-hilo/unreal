## Key Progression Patterns

### 1. **Difficulty Scales**
- **Early**: "Save $10" → **Mid**: "Track expenses" → **Late**: "Close first customer"
- **Early**: "Walk 10 minutes" → **Mid**: "Join fitness class" → **Late**: "Run 5k race"

### 2. **Specificity Increases**
- **Early**: Vague exploration ("Research SaaS idea")
- **Mid**: Concrete actions ("Interview one customer")
- **Late**: Commitment moves ("Quit current job", "Sign lease")

### 3. **Category Rotation**
- System prioritizes lowest-progress categories
- Completed categories (100%) are excluded from future tasks
- Ensures balanced growth across all areas

### 4. **Context Awareness**
- Tasks build on previous completions
- User feedback influences next day's tasks
- Recently completed tasks inform what comes next

### 5. **Milestone Shifts**
- **0-25%**: Awareness and small habits
- **25-50%**: Skill building and networking
- **50-75%**: Taking real action and making changes
- **75-100%**: Major commitments and execution
